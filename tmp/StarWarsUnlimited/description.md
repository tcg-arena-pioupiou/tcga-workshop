**Star Wars: Unlimited** (SWU) is a trading card game designed by Danny Schaefer and published by Fantasy Flight Games, launched in March 2024.

- **Players:** 2 to 4 (Twin Suns format for 3 to 4 players)
- **Updates:** new cards are added every Sunday

Thanks to [swu-db.com](https://www.swu-db.com/) for all the card data, without whom the game couldn't be played.
